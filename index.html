Connect Google Calendar

  1. 1

    Create Appointment Schedule

    In Google Calendar → click + → Appointment schedule. Set your working hours, buffer times, and slot duration.

  2. 2

    Copy Your Booking Link

    After saving, click View booking page. Copy the full URL — it ends with your Schedule ID.

  3. 3

    Update This Website

    In index.html, find YOUR_SCHEDULE_ID and replace it with your actual schedule URL. That's it!

Pro tip

Google Appointment Schedules automatically block out times when you're busy on your Google Calendar — so clients can only book slots you're actually free.
